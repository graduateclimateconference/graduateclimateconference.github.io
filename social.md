# Friday:

### Public Session:

BINGO!
- GCC attendees will be encouraged to play bingo to start to get to know their fellow attendees. The bingo sheet is linked here.
- We will print out enough of these sheets and we will hand them out at the check in table. When someone gets a bingo, we will collect the sheets (they need to put their full name on the bottom) so that we can draw a bingo winner on Saturday night. If they get a blackout, they get three entries for the prize! Yes, there will be a fun prize!

### Evening Activities (8:30pm):

Movie Night
- Location: Scott Hall
- We will be showing Halloweentown (keeping the Halloween/spooky season vibes going)
- Snacks & drinks will be available

Speed Peering/Game Night
- Location: Pack Hall
- The evening will start with an organized speed peering activity where people can get to know each other
- hen we will leave the evening open to board/card games
- If you have games you can bring for people to play, please do!!
- Snacks & drinks will be available

# Saturday: 

### Morning Hike (5:15am-8:00am)

Organized Hike: Little Mashel Falls
- Location: Dinning Hall
- No permit or fees required
- ~5.8 miles roundtrip
- ~728 feet elevation gain
- Time: 2.5-3 hours
- Hike map
- Meet at 5:00 am. Group will depart at 5:15am

### Evening Activities (9:15pm):

Halloween Party & Trivia Night
- Location: Pack Hall
- We will do our annual trivia! It will be done in teams and consist of several rounds. Prizes for winning team!
- Bingo winner will be drawn and receive their prize!
- Costume contest after bingo. Prizes will be handed out for best costumes!
- The rest of the evening will be mingling and enjoying snacks & drinks!
